About

I’m Pisek. I run AI at CJ Express Group, the Thai retail group behind CJ Supermarket and CJ More — 2,500 stores across Thailand. I run the AI Centre of Excellence at head office: a team of forward-deployed engineers embedded in the business, building computer vision, recommendation and agentic systems where the company actually operates.

Before that I did a PhD in electrical engineering at Toronto, on quantum photonics — entangled photon sources in semiconductor waveguides — then spent years designing optical systems for AR glasses and surgical robotics. Physics taught me to distrust any result I can’t measure, which is why I care more about evaluation harnesses than leaderboards.

The governance side exists for one reason: a four-layer model — prevention, gateway, observability, live protection — plus tool sanctioning and policy aligned to PDPA and ISO/IEC 42001. It’s what lets 500 people use what we build directly, and 2,500 colleagues across HQ work with AI, without anyone betting the company on it.
