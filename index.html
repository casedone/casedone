About

I’m Pisek. I lead AI at CJ Express, a Thai retail and logistics company, where my job is the unglamorous half of AI: deciding what to build, proving it works, and keeping it running after the demo.

Before that I did a PhD in engineering, and spent years on NLP and computer vision that never made it into a launch announcement. That background is why I care more about evaluation harnesses than model leaderboards.
